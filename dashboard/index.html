Release, privacy and interpretation notes

National forecast ribbons sum district interval bounds; their national coverage is not independently calibrated. Stocks and allocation scenarios use the latest planning origin, so historical date selections do not imply historical stock reconstructions. Threshold changes update daily signal flags and the separate weekly review line. Equity is the minimum fraction of each district’s population-proportional allocation share.
